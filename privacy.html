Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

This policy explains how QAFlex Studio (“we”, “us”, or “our”) handles information when you visit this website, use its browser-based tools, contact us, subscribe to updates, or follow links to third-party services.

Information you provide

If you contact us by email, we receive the information you choose to include, such as your email address, name if provided, and message. Please do not send passwords, access tokens, confidential code, sensitive personal information, or production data.

Browser-based tools

Our free tools are designed to run in your browser. For tools that process text you enter locally, that text is not intentionally sent to a QAFlex Studio application server by the tool. However, the hosting platform and your browser may process technical information as part of serving the page. Review the notes on each tool and avoid entering confidential or sensitive material.

Email subscriptions

The newsletter signup form is provided by Brevo and embedded on this website. If you subscribe, the information you submit is processed by Brevo to manage your subscription and send requested emails. Follow the form's confirmation process and use its unsubscribe option to stop receiving messages. Brevo's own privacy terms also apply.

This website may link to or embed services such as Gumroad for digital product checkout and Brevo for email subscriptions. When you interact with those services, they may collect information under their own privacy policies, terms, and cookie practices. We do not control the independent privacy practices of third parties.

Cookies, analytics, and advertising

Third-party embeds or linked services may use cookies or similar technologies under their own policies. If analytics or advertising services are added to this website in the future, this policy should be updated to describe them and any relevant choices. Do not assume this site provides a personalised advertising or analytics opt-out unless the relevant control is made available.

How information is used

  • To respond to enquiries and provide requested support.
  • To deliver email updates when you subscribe.
  • To help maintain website functionality and address misuse or technical problems.
  • To meet applicable legal obligations where required.

Retention and security

Email correspondence may be retained for as long as reasonably needed to respond, maintain records, or meet legal obligations. No internet transmission or storage method is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your choices and requests

You can unsubscribe from newsletter emails using the unsubscribe option in the message. To ask about personal information contained in an enquiry, or to request correction or deletion where applicable, contact qaflex121@gmail.com. We may need to retain some records where required by law or for legitimate recordkeeping.

Children

This website is intended for general software testing and developer audiences, not specifically for children. Please do not knowingly submit children's personal information.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when website features or applicable requirements change. The latest revision date will be shown at the top of this page.

Contact
